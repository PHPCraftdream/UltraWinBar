## Decisions
- Removed Explorer's start-up delay with two HKCU Serialize values rather than a Task Scheduler logon task: no admin, reversible, and a task could start before Explorer's shell window exists, so the app would take itself for the shell.
- Announce the tray (TaskbarCreated) after Explorer's icons are read on a background thread, guarded by a self-announcement flag, rather than leaving it synchronous or letting ExplorerMonitor see our own broadcast.
- Size task buttons from the scroll viewer's viewport width and skip sizing while the width is unknown, rather than re-sizing from every list's SizeChanged.
- Four separate commits per concern (cleanup, order, reinstall script, start-up), partial CHANGELOG variants staged through hash-object.
- For the unexplained reordering, add an audit trail instead of guessing at a fix.
